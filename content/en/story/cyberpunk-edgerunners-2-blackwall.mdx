## What Is the Blackwall? A Primer for Newcomers

If you skipped *Cyberpunk 2077* and went straight to the anime, here's the short version. The Blackwall (sometimes written Black Wall) is a firewall built by NetWatch and housed inside the Net. Its purpose is simple to describe and horrifying to think about: keep rogue, superintelligent AIs from crossing into the physical world.

It is not a wall you can walk up to and knock on. It is a border made of code, patrolled by an agency that understands exactly how fragile it is, and every netrunner who dives deep enough eventually feels it watching back.

| Term | What it is | Why it matters to the story |
|---|---|---|
| The Blackwall | NetWatch-built firewall inside the Net | Keeps rogue AI out of the real world |
| Rogue AI | Superintelligent programs beyond the wall | The quiet threat everyone in Night City fears |
| NetWatch | International net-security agency | Builds and patrols the Blackwall |
| Netrunner | Hacker who dives into the Net | Meets the wall every time they go deep |
| Soulkiller | Arasaka program tied to digitized minds | Feeds the "who counts as human" question |
| Phantom Liberty | *Cyberpunk 2077* expansion | Pushed Blackwall lore into the main plot |

For a series built on intimate, street-level tragedy, the Blackwall is a big escalation. It moves the danger from a corpo boardroom to the edge of human comprehension.
