Night City is coming back to Netflix, but the skyline fans memorized in 2022 is only part of the story. The Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting has been confirmed as the same dystopian world as *Cyberpunk 2077* — yet everything happening inside it is brand new.

That distinction is the most important thing to understand about the sequel. The Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting tells you exactly what you're getting: no recycled tragedy, no retread of David Martinez's fall, and a fresh crew of edgerunners carving their own path through the most dangerous city of the dark future.

CD PROJEKT RED announced the project during its Anime Expo panel in Los Angeles, confirming that it reunited with animation studio TRIGGER for a standalone series that will stream on Netflix. You can read the [official *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* announcement from CD PROJEKT RED](https://press.cdprojektred.com/en/news/1786/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-officially-announced) for the primary details.

## Why the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Setting Matters

Setting is never wallpaper in this franchise. Night City is a pressure cooker: corporate towers, gang-run blocks, bootleg chrome clinics, and a skyline that sells dreams it has no intention of honoring. Where a story plants its characters determines what kind of story it can tell.

When showrunner, story writer, and producer Bartosz Sztybor described the new project, he framed it as a raw, realistic chronicle of redemption and revenge, and noted that while David's story may be over, there is plenty more to discover in Night City. That framing does a lot of work. It confirms the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting stays in the same city, keeps the same continuity, and swaps the cast rather than the map.

For viewers, that means the world-building you already absorbed — fixers, ripperdocs, braindance clubs, the ever-present threat of cyberpsychosis — carries over intact. What changes is the lens. A new protagonist means a new neighborhood, a new class of enemies, and a different slice of the city's economy to survive.

### The Announcement at a Glance

The reveal happened during the livestreamed Anime Expo panel *"Cyberpunk: Edgerunners — Behind the Scenes With its Creators,"* which featured Sztybor alongside voice actors Emi Lo (Lucy Kushinada) and Zach Aguilar (David Martinez), hosted by Joey "The Anime Man" Bizinger. ## New Cast, New Rules: How the Setting Reshapes the Story

The original series worked because it was a tragedy of upward mobility — a kid with too much chrome and too much heart climbing toward a skyline that was never going to let him stay. A redemption-and-revenge premise suggests a different emotional engine: a character who has already lost something and is now deciding what to do about it.

### Why Redemption and Revenge Fit Night City

Revenge is a classic cyberpunk motivator because the genre assumes that institutions are too big to fix and too corrupt to appeal to. Redemption is the harder half of the pairing, and it is what keeps a revenge plot from becoming hollow. A character seeking both is a character making choices — and choices are what Night City punishes best.

That combination also suits an anthology-style structure. Because the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting does not depend on David's arc, the writers can build a self-contained emotional arc across a single season without worrying about inherited obligations.

## How the Setting Connects to Cyberpunk 2077 and the Tabletop Lore

The anime does not exist in a vacuum. It sits inside a universe that began with Mike Pondsmith's tabletop roleplaying games and expanded into *Cyberpunk 2077*. Night City itself has in-universe history — a planned corporate utopia that collapsed into the sprawl players and viewers know — and that history is the reason the city feels like a place rather than a backdrop.

For anyone preparing to watch, a little groundwork goes a long way. The table below outlines a practical prep path.

| Step | What to Do | Why It Helps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Watch the 2022 series | Establishes tone, pacing, and how the anime handles cyberpsychosis |
| 2 | Play or read up on *Cyberpunk 2077* | Fills in fixers, gangs, districts, and corporate politics |
| 3 | Skim the Edgerunners content in the game | Shows how the anime was folded into the game world |
| 4 | Revisit the official announcement | Keeps expectations anchored to confirmed facts |

The key takeaway is that the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting inherits a dense, well-documented world. Writers can drop a viewer into a district and trust that the politics, economy, and danger level are already understood.

## FAQ

**Is the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting the same Night City as the first season?**
Yes. The series is described as a standalone story set in the world of *Cyberpunk 2077*, which means the same city, the same continuity, and the same rules — just a different cast and a different storyline.

**Will David Martinez appear in Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2?**
The official announcement describes a brand-new cast of characters and states that David's story is over. Returning characters have not been confirmed either way, so treat any cameo claims as unverified until an official source says otherwise.

**Does the new setting mean I need to watch the first season?**
No. It is being built as a standalone series, so newcomers can start fresh. That said, watching the 2022 season first will make the world feel richer and clarify the tone the new team is working within.

**When does Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 release?**
No release date has been announced. The project was revealed at Anime Expo with logo, poster art, and teaser footage, but CD PROJEKT RED has not shared a launch window, so any specific date circulating online is speculation.

The Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 setting gives the franchise something rare: a second chance to explore Night City without repeating itself. Same city, new wounds.
