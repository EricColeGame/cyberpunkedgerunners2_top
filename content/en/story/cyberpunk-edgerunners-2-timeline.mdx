## Three Realistic Scenarios for the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Timeline

With the baseline set, the possibilities collapse into three broad buckets. Each has real upsides and real storytelling risks.

### Scenario 1: A Prequel Set Before 2076

A prequel opens up Night City at a different stage of its decay, with a cast that shares no baggage with season 1. The upside is total creative freedom and zero continuity traps. The downside is emotional: no Lucy, no Falco, and no payoff for the ending viewers are still recovering from.

### Scenario 2: A Parallel Story Between 2076 and 2077

This is where the canon is densest and the fan interest is highest. A parallel story could follow a different crew reacting to the same corporate warfare, or show how Night City absorbed the aftermath of the Arasaka Tower incident. It's also the riskiest option, because every date has to line up with established events.

### Scenario 3: A Sequel Set After 2077

A sequel would let the story move into genuinely unexplored territory — post-2077 Night City, where the game's multiple endings complicate what counts as canon. It's the most exciting option on paper and the hardest to execute without stepping on the game's toes.

| Scenario | In-Universe Window | Biggest Advantage | Biggest Risk | Community Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prequel | Before 2076 | Blank slate for new characters | No emotional continuity | Moderate |
| Parallel | 2076–2077 | Richest existing lore | Continuity errors | Strong |
| Sequel | After 2077 | Fresh, unexplored era | Conflicts with game endings | Strong |

Community reports lean hardest toward the parallel option, but that's sentiment rather than evidence. No leak has been corroborated by the studio, and official messaging about "new characters" cuts against a straight continuation.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Timeline

**When does Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 take place?**
No official in-universe year has been announced. Season 1 is set in 2076, and the most likely windows for a follow-up are either a parallel story between 2076 and 2077 or a sequel set after the events of Cyberpunk 2077.

**Is season 2 a sequel or a prequel?**
Unconfirmed. Official messaging has emphasized new characters and a new story, which suggests a standalone entry rather than a direct continuation — but that could still be set before, during, or after season 1.

**Do I need to play Cyberpunk 2077 to follow the story?**
No, but it helps. Season 1 works as a self-contained tragedy. Playing the game adds context about Night City's politics, the megacorps, and the small in-game tributes to the anime's crew.

**Will any season 1 characters return?**
Only Lucy and Falco are positioned to appear in a post-2076 setting without a flashback. A prequel would open the door to earlier versions of the whole crew, which is part of why that theory stays popular in the community.
