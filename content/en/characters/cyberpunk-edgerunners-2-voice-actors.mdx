## Meet the Four Leads Voiced in Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2

Each of the four announced characters comes with a short official description that hints at their arc. Pairing those descriptions with the actors attached to them makes the casting logic much clearer.

### Weak Kingsley — Kentaro Tone (JP) / Clancy Brown (EN)

Weak was once known as "King," a veteran edgerunner at the top of his game. Now he lives in the shadow of that former glory, forced to operate without chrome while searching for a sense of purpose in a world that has passed him by. Kentaro Tone, whose credits include Haku in *Utawarerumono: Mask of Truth*, voices him in Japanese. Clancy Brown takes the English dub — a marquee name whose presence signals that the production wants weight and authority behind this role.

### D — Koki Uchiyama (JP) / Nazeeh Tarsha (EN)

D is a Snake Nation netrunner with sharp technical skills and an even sharper hunger for revenge. He is hunting the killer who wiped out his clan, and that hunt pulls him toward corpo secrets that were never meant to surface. Koki Uchiyama, known to anime fans as Shigaraki in *My Hero Academia*, handles the Japanese performance. Nazeeh Tarsha voices him in English, taking on what looks like the season's most aggressive lead.

### Roman Carax — Momoka Terasawa (JP) / Valeria Rodriguez (EN)

Roman is the outsider's lens on Night City: a young cinephile chasing real stories in a city that has abandoned film for braindance. Obsessed with documenting the truth, he shadows other people's lives — only to discover that unlike his favorite reels, Night City's stories are messy, brutal, and rarely resolved cleanly. Momoka Terasawa (*Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines!*) voices him in Japanese, with Valeria Rodriguez in the English dub.

### Talia Yang — Akari Kito (JP) / Kimoy Lee (EN)

Talia was raised in corpo towers but hardened inside Maelstrom's grip. She is fiercely independent and steeped in the gang's hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture. Akari Kito — Nezuko in *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba* — voices her in Japanese, while Kimoy Lee takes the English dub. Talia reads as the wildcard of the group: someone whose loyalties are entirely her own.

### The Mysterious Fifth Character

Given the four leads already cover legacy, revenge, observation, and belonging, the fifth slot could be anything from an antagonist to a catalyst who ties the four storylines together. Expect that reveal to be the next major casting beat before launch.

Dual-cast announcements always trigger the same debate: sub or dub? The honest answer is that both versions are being built with intent, and the credits on each side tell you what the production is going for.

A few practical notes for viewers planning their first watch:

- **Watch Japanese first if** you want the original performance timing, which is what the animation was cut to.
- **Watch the English dub first if** you are coming off the first season's dub and want continuity of tone.
- **Both are worth a rewatch.** A ten-episode season is short enough that a second pass in the other language is genuinely feasible.
- **Dub casting is confirmed, not rumored.** These names came from the official announcement, so there is no speculation involved.

## What the Casting Reveals About the Story

Four characters, four narrative jobs. Reading the official descriptions side by side shows how tightly the season is engineered.

| Character | Narrative Function | Core Theme |
|---|---|---|
| Weak | The fallen legend seeking purpose | Legacy and obsolescence |
| D | The engine of revenge | Family and loss |
| Roman | The observer documenting others | Truth versus entertainment |
| Talia | The unpredictable insider | Identity and belonging |

The official synopsis ties all four together around family, obsession, and legacy — plus the idea that "how your lens reflects the stories of the people around you" is itself part of the story. Roman literally carries a lens, which suggests he is the audience surrogate: the character through whom we learn what the others are hiding.

That thematic density is why the casting matters more than usual here. A revenge arc lives or dies on how much pain the performer can imply without shouting it. A fallen-legend arc needs someone who can play exhaustion and dignity at the same time. If the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 voice actors were miscast, the writing would collapse. Based on the credits attached, that risk looks low.

## FAQ

**Who are the main Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 voice actors?**

The four confirmed leads are Kentaro Tone and Clancy Brown as Weak Kingsley, Koki Uchiyama and Nazeeh Tarsha as D, Momoka Terasawa and Valeria Rodriguez as Roman Carax, and Akari Kito and Kimoy Lee as Talia Yang. Each character has one Japanese performer and one English dub performer, with a fifth character still unannounced.

**Do I need to watch the first Cyberpunk: Edgerunners before this season?**

No. Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 is described as a standalone story with a new cast of characters, set in the same Night City but not a direct continuation. Watching the first season is still worthwhile for context on the world, the gangs, and how braindance and chrome work.

**When and where can I watch Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2?**

It is slated to stream exclusively on Netflix in Fall 2026, with a ten-episode run. Studio TRIGGER is handling animation, with Kai Ikarashi directing.

**Is there a fifth character in Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2?**

Yes — the anime's official website teases a mysterious fifth character. No name, visual, or voice actor has been confirmed yet, so expect that announcement to land before the season premieres.
