## Why the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Cast Reveal Matters

The Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 cast is officially locked in, and it signals a clean break from the crew fans mourned back in 2022. Instead of resurrecting David, Lucy, and Rebecca, studio TRIGGER is building a ten-episode standalone story around four brand-new leads. For anyone who wondered whether a sequel could ever live up to the original, the real question has shifted: can this new Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 cast carry Night City on its own?

That question carries weight because the first *Edgerunners* wasn't a throwaway tie-in. Directed by Hiroyuki Imaishi, it took the world of CD PROJEKT RED's *Cyberpunk 2077* and R. Talsorian Games' tabletop RPG, then won Anime of the Year at the 2023 Anime Awards. Any follow-up inherits that legacy — and a fanbase with very strong opinions about chrome, tragedy, and neon.

The cast announcement, which followed a teaser trailer, gave us names, faces, and the voice actors bringing them to life in both Japanese and English. Here's everything confirmed so far.

That ensemble structure means the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 cast isn't carrying one arc. It's carrying four that reportedly entangle to the extreme.

## Character Deep Dive: Who Are the New Leads?

### Weak Kingsley: The Fallen King

Weak was once known simply as "King," a veteran edgerunner at the top of his game. Now he lives in the shadow of that former glory, and — crucially — he's doing it without chrome. In a setting where augmentation is status, identity, and armor all at once, a legend stripped of his implants is a quietly brutal premise. His arc appears to be about purpose: what do you do when the world has moved past you and the tools that defined you are gone?

### D: The Netrunner Hunting a Killer

D is a Snake Nation netrunner with sharp technical skills and an even sharper appetite for revenge. He's tracking the person who wiped out his clan — a hunt that, according to the official synopsis, pulls him dangerously close to corporate secrets that were never meant to surface. If season one taught viewers anything, it's that corpo secrets in Night City are never worth the price of finding them.

### Roman Carax: The Filmmaker in a Braindance World

Roman is the most conceptually interesting addition to the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 cast. He's a young cinephile obsessed with documenting real stories in a city that has traded film for braindance. He shadows other people's lives, chasing authenticity — and he's about to learn that real stories don't wrap up neatly like the reels he loves. Expect him to function as both narrator-adjacent observer and emotional anchor.

### Talia Yang: Raised in Towers, Hardened by Maelstrom

Talia grew up in corporate towers but was hardened inside Maelstrom's grip, and she carries both influences. She's fiercely independent and fully immersed in the gang's hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture. Of the four, she's the one most likely to complicate the group's morality — someone who understands corpo polish and street brutality equally well.

## Behind the Scenes: The Team Shaping the New Cast

A cast is only half the equation. The staff list explains why this sequel looks and sounds the way it does.

| Role | Name | Notable Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Director | Kai Ikarashi | *Little Witch Academia* (animation director) |
| Series Composition | Masahiko Otsuka | *Promare* |
| Character Design | Ichigo Kanno | *Delicious in Dungeon* (key animator) |
| Music | Tsuneo Imahori | *Trigun* |
| Animation Studio | TRIGGER | Original *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners*, *Promare* |

Kai Ikarashi stepping into the director's chair is the biggest shift. Imaishi's style is maximalist and explosive; Ikarashi comes from a precise animation background. That doesn't mean the sequel will be quiet — the teaser was described as frenetic — but it does suggest a different visual rhythm.

Tsuneo Imahori on music is a promising detail for longtime anime fans. His work on *Trigun* blended western twang with sci-fi tension, a combination that suits Night City's desert sprawl surprisingly well.

## FAQ

**Who is in the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 cast?**

The four confirmed leads are Weak Kingsley, D, Roman Carax, and Talia Yang. Japanese voice actors are Kentaro Tone, Koki Uchiyama, Momoka Terasawa, and Akari Kito. The English dub features Clancy Brown, Nazeeh Tarsha, Valeria Rodriguez, and Kimoy Lee.

**Are David, Lucy, or any original characters returning?**

There's no confirmation of that. The sequel is described as a standalone ten-episode story, and the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 cast is entirely new. A fifth character has been teased but not revealed.

**Will there be an English dub?**

Yes. English dub voice actors were announced alongside the Japanese lineup, so both tracks are in production.

**When does Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 release?**

It arrives on Netflix in Fall 2026 as a ten-episode standalone series. An exact premiere date hasn't been announced.

**Do I need to watch the first season first?**

No. The new season is written as a standalone story with a fresh ensemble, so it works as an entry point — though the original is worth watching for context on Night City's rules.
