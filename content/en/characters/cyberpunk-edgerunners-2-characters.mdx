## Meet the Four Leads of Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2

Each of these characters carries a distinct fantasy — the fallen hero, the avenger, the observer, and the survivor. Here's what the official descriptions tell us about how they fit into the story.

### Weak Kingsley: The Legend Who Lost His Edge

Once known simply as "King," Weak was a veteran edgerunner operating at the very top of his game. Now he lives in the shadow of that former glory, and the story description adds a crucial detail: he's been forced to live *without chrome*, searching for purpose in a world that has already moved past him.

In Night City, losing your cyberware is close to losing your identity. That makes Weak the emotional anchor of the season — a man measuring himself against a version of himself that no longer exists.

### D: The Snake Nation Netrunner Out for Blood

D is a nomad netrunner from Snake Nation with sharp technical skills and an even sharper hunger for revenge. His stated goal is brutally simple: hunt down the killer who wiped out his clan.

Netrunners are the ghosts of the Cyberpunk universe, and pairing that skillset with a nomad's outsider perspective is a smart twist. D's path reportedly leads him uncomfortably close to corporate secrets that were never meant to surface.

### Roman Carax: The Cinephile Chasing Truth

Roman Carax is a young film obsessive living in a city that has abandoned cinema in favor of braindance. He's fixated on documenting reality, shadowing other people's lives with his lens — only to discover that unlike his favorite reels, Night City's stories are messy, brutal, and rarely resolved cleanly.

Roman functions as the audience's viewpoint character. Through his camera, the season asks how the stories we choose to record shape the people we become.

### Talia Yang: Corpo Roots, Maelstrom Hardened

Talia Yang was raised in corporate towers but hardened inside Maelstrom's grip. She is fiercely independent and steeped in the gang's hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture — a combination that makes her the most unpredictable member of the group.

Maelstrom's presence is a direct line back to the wider *Cyberpunk 2077* world, so expect Talia's arc to carry the heaviest dose of gang politics and body-modification horror.

## The Teased Fifth Character and Night City Connections

The official website has hinted at a fifth character who has not yet been revealed. No name, no artwork, no voice actor — just a tease. That absence is doing a lot of narrative work, because a ten-episode standalone series with four announced leads has room for exactly one more major presence.

Based on community reports, the most common theories are that the fifth figure ties directly to Weak's past as "King," or that they represent the corporate side of the story that D is busy dragging into the light. Neither theory is confirmed, and it's worth treating all of it as speculation until TRIGGER and Netflix say otherwise.

What we *can* say is that the character roster is deliberately built to touch several corners of the Cyberpunk universe at once:

- **Maelstrom**, through Talia Yang, brings gang violence and extreme body modification.
- **Snake Nation**, through D, connects the story to the nomad clans that live outside Night City's walls.
- **Braindance**, referenced in Roman's description, ties the season to the entertainment technology at the heart of the franchise.
- **Chrome and cyberware**, through Weak's chrome-free existence, reframes the series' core question: what's left of you when the hardware is gone?

That's a smart spread. Instead of retelling the first season's rise-and-fall arc, the new cast lets the writers explore legacy from four angles at once — the person who had it, the person who lost it, the person who wants to avenge it, and the person who just wants to record it before it disappears.

## FAQ About Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Characters

**Who are the main Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 characters?**
The confirmed main cast consists of four leads: Weak Kingsley, a former legend now living without chrome; D, a Snake Nation netrunner seeking revenge; Roman Carax, a young cinephile documenting Night City; and Talia Yang, a Maelstrom-hardened independent operator. A fifth character has been teased but not yet revealed.

**Is *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* a sequel to the first season?**
No. It's described as a standalone ten-episode story set in the same Night City, with a new cast of characters. You don't need to have watched the first season to follow it, though returning fans will recognize the world, the gangs, and the themes.

**Who voices Weak Kingsley in the English dub?**
Clancy Brown voices Weak in the English version, while Kentaro Tone handles the Japanese performance. It's one of the more notable dub castings of the season.

**When and where can I watch *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2*?**
The series is set to stream exclusively on Netflix in Fall 2026, running ten episodes. Check the official site and Netflix listings for the exact premiere date in your region.
