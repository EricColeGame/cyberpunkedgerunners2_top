Night City is getting four brand-new faces — and none of them are a retread of David Martinez. The **Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 new characters** were officially unveiled on June 30, 2026, just one day after a frenetic teaser trailer put the fandom back on high alert. Why does that matter? Because this **Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2** cast is the clearest sign yet that studio TRIGGER is building a standalone story rather than a straight sequel — and the four leads tell you exactly what kind of heartbreak you're signing up for.

Below, we break down every confirmed character, the Japanese and English voice actors behind them, the mysterious fifth slot teased on the official site, and what the production team has lined up for the ten-episode run heading to Netflix in Fall 2026.

## Who Are the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 New Characters?

The official synopsis describes Night City as a place where "lives become entangled to the extreme," and the cast reveal backs that promise up. Rather than following one protagonist, the story braids four perspectives together: a legend on the way down, a nomad hunting a killer, a filmmaker chasing truth, and a gang-hardened survivor.

That ensemble structure is a real departure from the first season's tighter focus. Here's the confirmed lineup at a glance.

| Character | Role / Archetype | Affiliation | Core Conflict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weak Kingsley** | Veteran edgerunner, fallen legend | Former top-tier solo known as "King" | Living without chrome in a world that passed him by |
| **D** | Netrunner with a revenge mission | Snake Nation | Hunting the killer who wiped out his clan |
| **Roman Carax** | Young cinephile and documentarian | Independent | Chasing real stories in a city that traded film for braindance |
| **Talia Yang** | Fiercely independent street operator | Maelstrom, via corpo-tower upbringing | Surviving a hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed gang culture |

### Weak Kingsley

Once known as "King," Weak was an edgerunner at the very top of his game. Now he lives in the shadow of that former glory — and, crucially, without chrome. In a setting where hardware is identity, a legend who can no longer afford implants is a walking ghost.

He's voiced by Kentaro Tone (Haku in *Utawarerumono: Mask of Truth*) in Japanese, with Clancy Brown handling the English dub. That English casting alone signals how much weight the show puts on this character.

### D

D is a Snake Nation netrunner with sharp technical skills and an even sharper appetite for revenge. He is hunting the killer who wiped out his clan, and the official description hints that his path drags him uncomfortably close to corpo secrets that were never meant to surface.

Koki Uchiyama (Shigaraki in *My Hero Academia*) voices him in Japanese, while Nazeeh Tarsha voices him in English. If the season has a pure engine of momentum, D is it.

### Roman Carax

Roman Carax is the odd one out: a young cinephile chasing real stories in a city that abandoned film for braindance. Obsessed with documenting the truth, he shadows other people's lives — only to learn that unlike his favorite reels, Night City's stories are messy, brutal, and rarely tied up with a clean ending.

Momoka Terasawa (Chika in *Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines!*) voices him in Japanese, with Valeria Rodriguez in the English dub. Roman is also the character most likely to function as the audience's lens.

### Talia Yang

Raised in corpo towers but hardened in Maelstrom's grip, Talia is fiercely independent and steeped in the gang's hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture. That background makes her the most contradictory member of the crew — corpo polish layered over gang brutality.

Akari Kito (Nezuko in *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba*) voices her in Japanese, with Kimoy Lee in the English dub.

## How Season 2 Compares to Season 1

The Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 new characters aren't the only change — the entire creative pipeline has shifted.

| Aspect | Season 1 | Season 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Director | Hiroyuki Imaishi | Kai Ikarashi |
| Structure | Single-protagonist crew story | Ten-episode standalone ensemble |
| Central Figures | David Martinez and his crew | Weak, D, Roman Carax, Talia Yang |
| Setting | Night City | Night City |
| Streaming | Netflix | Netflix, Fall 2026 |
| Awards | Anime of the Year, 2023 Anime Awards | To be determined |

The most important difference is narrative shape. Season 1 built toward a single inevitable tragedy. Season 2, per its own synopsis, is about family, obsession, and legacy — and about how your lens reflects the stories of the people around you. That's a theme-driven premise, not a plot-driven one.
