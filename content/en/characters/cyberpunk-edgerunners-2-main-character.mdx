## Meet the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Main Character Lineup

Night City is about to get a brand-new crew, and the biggest question on every fan's mind is simple: who stands at the center of it? The Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 main character lineup was officially announced on June 30, 2026 — one day after a frenetic teaser trailer dropped — and the reveal confirms something important. This is an ensemble story, not a solo hero's journey. Instead of one obvious lead, the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 main character roster spreads the spotlight across four very different people.

Why does that matter? The original *Edgerunners*, directed by Hiroyuki Imaishi at studio TRIGGER, became one of the most talked-about anime of its year and took home Anime of the Year at the 2023 Anime Awards. Following that with a single new protagonist would have been the safe play. Instead, TRIGGER and CD PROJEKT RED are building a ten-episode standalone series around a group whose lives collide "to the extreme," as the official synopsis puts it.

## Weak Kingsley: The Legend Who Lost His Chrome

Weak Kingsley is the closest thing this series has to a traditional focal point, and his premise is a cyberpunk tragedy running in reverse. He was once known as "King," a veteran edgerunner operating at the very top of his game. Now he lives in the shadow of that former glory, and the story forces him to get by without chrome in a world that has already moved past him.

That inversion is what makes him interesting. In a genre where power usually means more implants, Weak's arc is about losing them. He isn't chasing a bigger gun or a better deck — he's chasing a reason to keep going. According to the official description, he's searching for a sense of purpose rather than revenge, which puts him at odds with almost everyone else in the cast.

| Weak Kingsley at a Glance | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Former alias | "King" |
| Status | Washed-up edgerunner legend |

Casting Kentaro Tone in Japanese and Clancy Brown in English signals the kind of weight the production wants behind the character. Both are voices associated with presence and authority — exactly what a fallen legend needs. If you're new to the franchise, this is a smart entry point: Weak's story is self-contained, and you don't need to have watched the first series to follow it.

## D, Roman Carax, and Talia Yang: The Rest of the Core Four

The remaining three leads each represent a different lens on Night City — revenge, truth, and independence. Together they give the series its stated themes: family, obsession, and legacy.

### D: Snake Nation's Netrunner on a Revenge Path

D is a netrunner tied to Snake Nation, and the official description gives him sharp technical skills and an even sharper hunger for revenge. He is hunting the person who wiped out his clan, and that hunt pulls him uncomfortably close to corporate secrets that were never meant to surface.

Mechanically, a netrunner lead changes the texture of the action. Where the first series leaned heavily on brute-force chrome and firepower, D's toolkit is information — breaching, tracking, and turning systems against their owners. Expect his arc to intersect with corpo intrigue rather than street-level brawls.

### Roman Carax: The Cinephile Chasing Real Stories

Roman Carax is the wild card. He's a young film obsessive living in a city that has largely abandoned cinema in favor of braindance. Convinced that real stories still matter, he shadows other people's lives to document the truth — and slowly discovers that Night City's narratives are messy, brutal, and rarely wrapped up with a clean ending.

That framing gives the series a built-in narrator figure without making him the protagonist. Roman is the audience's camera, and the show's promotional material leans into that idea of "how your lens reflects the stories of the people around you."

### Talia Yang: Corpo Raised, Maelstrom Hardened

Talia Yang grew up in corporate towers but was forged inside Maelstrom's grip. She is fiercely independent and fully immersed in the gang's hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture. She's the character most likely to remind viewers that Night City doesn't offer clean moral choices — only expensive ones.

## FAQ: Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 Main Character Questions

**Who is the main character of Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2?**
There isn't a single lead. The series is built as an ensemble, with Weak Kingsley, D, Roman Carax, and Talia Yang sharing the spotlight. Weak gets the most setup in promotional material, so he's the closest thing to a primary protagonist, but the story is explicitly framed around how all four lives become entangled.

**Do I need to watch the first Edgerunners series before this one?**
No. The new series is described as a standalone ten-episode story set in the same Night City, so prior knowledge isn't required. Returning viewers will recognize the setting, the tech, and the tone, but the cast is entirely new.

**When and where can I watch Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2?**
It streams exclusively on Netflix with a Fall 2026 release window. The teaser trailer arrived on June 29, 2026, followed by the main cast announcement on June 30, 2026.

**Who voices the Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 main character cast in English?**
Clancy Brown voices Weak Kingsley, Nazeeh Tarsha voices D, Valeria Rodriguez voices Roman Carax, and Kimoy Lee voices Talia Yang. The Japanese cast includes Kentaro Tone, Koki Uchiyama, Momoka Terasawa, and Akari Kito.
