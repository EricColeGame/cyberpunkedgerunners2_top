### VTK vs. ksUV at a Glance

Both are widely used community body frameworks, and neither is "better" in the abstract — the right answer is whichever one the rest of your load order depends on.

| Framework | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| VTK | Players whose existing clothing and body mods are already VTK-based | Mixing in ksUV assets causes visible seams and stretching |
| ksUV | Players with a ksUV-centric load order | Same conflict risk in reverse; textures may not map cleanly |
| Switching between them | Only when you are willing to rebuild your mod list | Expect to reinstall clothing and skin mods after a swap |

If you are starting from a clean install with no strong preference, decide based on the clothing mods you care about most, then commit. Consistency beats raw quality every time.

## Tips for Building a Screen-Accurate Talia Yang V

Once the body mesh is in place, the rest of the look comes down to your character creator choices and your photo-mode habits. These are the steps that consistently get players closest to the community's reference shots.

- **Match the silhouette first, then the face.** Body proportions drive recognition more than facial sliders do. A vanilla body-size refit is designed to preserve those proportions, so resist the urge to stack additional body-shaping mods on top.
- **Keep cyberware visible.** The design language leans on exposed chrome. Choose cyberware placements that read clearly in third-person and in photo mode rather than ones hidden under jackets.
- **Layer clothing with intention.** Because the mesh is a refit, fitted tops and jackets deform more cleanly than bulky outerwear. Test each outfit in motion, not just in the inventory screen.
- **Use photo mode lighting to check your work.** Neon rim light is flattering but hides clipping. Check your character under flat, neutral light before you commit.
- **Create a preset and save it.** Character creators are unforgiving. Save your sliders so a patch or a framework update does not send you back to square one.
- **Respect the mod's terms.** No reuploads, no conversions, and ask before modifying. Crediting the original creator is standard practice.

|---|---|---|

| Outfit | Fitted layers | Oversized jackets that clip |
