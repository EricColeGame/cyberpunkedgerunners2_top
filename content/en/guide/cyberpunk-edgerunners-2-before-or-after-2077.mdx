Here's the thing that trips people up: Cyberpunk isn't a franchise with one tidy timeline. It's a setting that has been explored across multiple decades, multiple mediums, and multiple points of view.

The original tabletop game established a world in the 2010s and 2020s. The Cyberpunk RED era pushed forward into the 2040s. The first Edgerunners season took place in 2076 — one year before the events of Cyberpunk 2077 — and ended in a way that dovetailed neatly into the game's opening.

| Era / Year | Primary media | Notes |

| TBD | Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 | Timeline unannounced |

That table is exactly why the question is so persistent. There's no single "present day" in this universe — there are at least four viable windows a new show could occupy, and each one would feel completely different.

## The Three Timeline Scenarios, Ranked

Community discussion across Steam forums, fan groups, and social media has largely settled into three camps. None of these are official, but each has a real logic behind it.

| Scenario | What it would look like | Why people believe it | Community traction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Before 2077** | A prequel set in the 2060s or early 2070s, showing a younger Night City | Season 1 proved the prequel formula works | High |
| **Around 2077** | A parallel story running alongside the game's events | Lets the show reference game events without spoiling them | Moderate |
| **After 2077** | A post-game Night City dealing with a new power vacuum | Fits the "completely different" promise and frees the writers from canon | High |
| **Outside Night City** | A different city entirely, in any era | Rumored in fan discussions as a way to expand the world | Speculative |

### Scenario 1: Before 2077

This is the safest bet structurally. Season 1 was a prequel, and it worked beautifully — audiences had no trouble investing in characters whose fate was already implied by the game's world state. A second prequel could explore the years between the RED era and 2077, a stretch of history that's relatively unexplored on screen.

### Scenario 2: Around 2077

An overlapping timeline is trickier than it sounds. If the story runs parallel to V's, the writers have to be careful about cause and effect — one character's actions can't accidentally break the game's plot. Still, it's an appealing option because it lets the show nod at familiar events without retelling them.

### Scenario 3: After 2077

The most creatively free option. Placing the story after the game's events means the show inherits a changed Night City without being bound by a specific save file or ending. It also aligns with the "something completely different" framing that CD Projekt Red used when discussing a potential follow-up.
