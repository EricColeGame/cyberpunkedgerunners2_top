### Why a brand-new cast makes sense

The original season told a complete, self-contained tragedy. Bringing back the same crew would undercut that ending, and it would also force the new story to live in the shadow of a beloved arc. A new ensemble gives the writers room to explore different corners of the same world — different fixers, different implants, different reasons to run.

Community reports suggest fans are split between wanting cameos and wanting a clean break. Both instincts are reasonable, but only one of them protects the new show's ability to stand on its own.
